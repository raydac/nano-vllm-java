# Little Red Riding Hood

Little Red Riding Hood is a fairy tale collected by the Brothers Grimm. It is a fairy tale, not a fable.

The German title of the tale is Rotkäppchen.

The story is also known in French as Le Petit Chaperon Rouge, from an earlier version by Charles Perrault.

The main characters are Little Red Riding Hood, her mother, her grandmother, the wolf, and the huntsman.

Little Red Riding Hood wears a little cap made of red velvet, which is why she got her name.

Her mother sends her through the woods with cake and wine for her sick grandmother.

The mother warns Little Red Riding Hood not to leave the path on the way to grandmother's house.

The grandmother lives in the woods, about a half hour from the village.

A wolf meets Little Red Riding Hood in the woods and asks where she is going.

The wolf tricks Little Red Riding Hood into picking flowers while he runs ahead to the grandmother's house.

The wolf eats the grandmother, puts on her clothes and cap, and lies in her bed.

Little Red Riding Hood notices the wolf's big ears, eyes, hands, and mouth in the famous dialogue in the bedroom.

The wolf then jumps from the bed and eats Little Red Riding Hood as well.

A huntsman hears loud snoring, finds the wolf in the bed, and cuts open the wolf's belly.

Little Red Riding Hood and the grandmother come out of the wolf alive.

They fill the wolf's body with heavy stones, and when he wakes he falls down dead.

At the end of the first tale, the huntsman takes the wolf's pelt, and the grandmother eats the cake and drinks the wine.

Little Red Riding Hood promises never to leave the path alone again if her mother forbids it.

In a second adventure in the same text, another wolf tries to lure her from the path, but she goes straight to
grandmother's house.

In that second ending, the wolf falls from the roof into a trough of water and sausage broth and drowns.

The wolf often symbolizes danger, deception, and the threat of the wild woods in readings of the tale.

The red hood or red cap is a famous costume detail that identifies the heroine.
